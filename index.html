About

mohokoto is a software engineer who spends most days moving between backend systems and small side experiments. Recent interests include distributed systems, generative art, and slowly digitizing a decade of paper notes. This site is where those threads eventually end up.

Projects

  • Tideline

    A minimal habit tracker built in a weekend, still running three years later.

  • Fieldnote

    A lightweight note-taking tool that syncs across devices without needing an account. Started as a way to avoid losing conference notes, it slowly grew into a daily writing habit.

  • Quietwork

    An ongoing experiment in async-first project management for small teams. Instead of real-time chat, updates route through structured, threaded posts that get summarized daily — an attempt to see whether a team can stay coordinated with meaningfully less synchronous communication. Still early and rough around the edges.
